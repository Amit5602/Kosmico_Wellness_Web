Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to Kosmiko Wellness ("we," "our," "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Age, gender, height, weight
  • Health information you choose to share, including but not limited to:
    • Blood pressure readings
    • Blood glucose / diabetes status
    • Dietary preferences and restrictions
    • Meal logs and food photos

1.2 Photos and Images

  • Images of food/meals you upload for AI-based nutrition analysis
  • These images may be processed by third-party AI services to identify food items and estimate nutritional content

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information
  • Usage data
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Log data and crash reports

1.4 Health Data Disclaimer

Any health-related information generated or displayed by the Service is for general wellness and informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • Analyze food images and generate nutritional estimates
  • Track health metrics
  • Personalize your experience
  • Communicate regarding updates and support
  • Monitor usage trends
  • Detect fraud and technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process your personal data based on:

  • Your consent
  • Legitimate interest
  • Legal obligation
